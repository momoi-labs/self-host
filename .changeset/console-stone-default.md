The console moves to Kiso's default look with the stone accent: square
frames with corner ticks, a soft shadow on the outermost panel only, and a
quiet solid background. Diagrams follow the same frames and corners.

Tables now have a single frame, like Kiso's Products list. A table inside a
card or a detail tab no longer draws a second border or shadow. A failure
Alert at the top of a detail screen no longer gets cut off.
