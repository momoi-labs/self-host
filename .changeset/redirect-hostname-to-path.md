A redirect can now send a hostname's root to a path, such as `admin.example.com` to `/admin`, by picking the hostname and typing the path; the pattern is written for you. Redirects show on the Summary diagram with a dashed line to the route they land on, and every route type has its own icon there and on the Routes tab: Hostname, Alias, Path and Redirect.
