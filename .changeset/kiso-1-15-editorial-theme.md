The console moves to Kiso 1.15 and a new look: editorial headings, hard
panel shadows, tick corner marks, a paper fibers background and an inset
shell that fills to the bottom and right edges.

A failed deploy, database operation, image build or virtual machine
operation now shows Kiso's loud error Alert, with an animated hazard tape,
the fix next to its action and the causes behind a "Show causes" toggle.
